# Clinical timeline

Yash’s clinical history, as summarized on the [project website](https://rnafoundry.com/yash/#clinical-history), with links to the available reports and [sample metadata](samples.tsv). Report dates are identified separately from treatment and specimen collection dates.

| When | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| October–November 2024 | Weight loss, fatigue, and abdominal and back pain began. | Yash’s account, summarized on the project website. |
| 14–17 January 2025 | Admitted to Stanford and diagnosed with metastatic pancreatic cancer. Liver biopsy on 15 January; FOLFIRINOX started on 17 January. | Project clinical history; [Stanford pathology/IHC](reports/stanford-pathology-ihc-2025-01.pdf) and [Caris tissue report](reports/caris-tissue-2025-02-22.pdf). |
| January 2025, first chemotherapy cycle | Anaemia required frequent blood transfusions. | Project clinical history. |
| 31 January 2025 | Stanford STAMP tumour genetic panel reported. | [STAMP report](reports/stanford-stamp-2025-01-31.pdf). |
| 22 February 2025 | Caris liver-tissue profiling reported the CDH1::NRG1 fusion. The specimen was collected on 15 January 2025. | [Caris tissue report](reports/caris-tissue-2025-02-22.pdf). |
| 5 March 2025 | CT: pancreatic tumour mildly smaller; liver and lung lesions stable to slightly smaller. Suspected peritoneal disease and small-volume ascites. | Project clinical history. |
| After five FOLFIRINOX cycles | Switched to zenocutuzumab (Bizengri), with a strong initial response and minimal side effects. CA19-9 fell from approximately 45,000 at diagnosis to 18. | Yash’s account, summarized on the project website; exact switch date not specified. |
| August 2025 | Rising CA19-9, growing lung nodules and a new peritoneal nodule. Liver and pancreatic disease remained controlled on zenocutuzumab. | Yash’s account, summarized on the project website. |
| October 2025 | Peritoneal biopsy yielded insufficient tissue. Liquid biopsy still detected the NRG1 fusion; zenocutuzumab continued through October. | Project clinical history; [Guardant360 report](reports/guardant360-2025-10-21.pdf), dated 21 October. |
| November 2025 | Started gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel plus afatinib in India. Rash limited afatinib use. Also receiving high-dose vitamin C infusions outside standard treatment. | Yash’s account, summarized on the project website. |
| 30 October 2026 | Matched-normal white-blood-cell DNA sample collected. | [Sample metadata](samples.tsv); date supplied by the project team. |
