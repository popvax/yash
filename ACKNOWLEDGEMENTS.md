## GTEx

The data used for the analyses described here were obtained indirectly from
the GTEx Portal through the recorded SNAF/LENS normal-control matrices.
The original Portal download date is not recorded. Source files and hashes
are identified in the normal-screening evidence.

These historical derived controls do not reflect the most current/accurate data
available from the GTEx Portal. No GTEx endorsement is implied.
