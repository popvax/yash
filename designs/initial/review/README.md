# Source-level antigen evidence

[Current antigen evidence](antigen-evidence.json) records the 30 selected regions.
The readable assessment is [antigen-review.md](../antigen-review.md).

Raw read-review requests, the executed worker, results and tumour allele-balance
tables are source measurements. They can cover additional loci or windows beyond
the final selection; they are not another vaccine design. The selected TSV is the
authoritative antigen list. Splice selection and normal screening replay from
[data/shared-rna/splicing](../../../data/shared-rna/splicing/README.md).
In-frame fusion evidence is in [data/shared-rna/fusions](../../../data/shared-rna/fusions/README.md).
