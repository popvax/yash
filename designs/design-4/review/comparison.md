# How Design 4 compares with Design 3 and Design 2

[Design 3](../../design-3/README.md) (`30` regions, `885 aa`, `2,655 nt` sense coding) is PopVax's current lead candidate, and [Design 2](../../design-2/README.md) (`24` regions, `724 aa`, `2,172 nt` sense coding) is the earlier matched-normal baseline. Design 4 main (`16` regions, `507 aa`, `1,521 nt` sense coding) shares **13 exact `region_id`s** (byte-identical 25-aa sequences) with Design 3: 11 paired-DNA/RNA mutations from Design 2 (`KLF5`, `SF1`, `AQP3`, `RXRA`, `HSPG2`, `TGFBR2`, `PPIC`, `VPS11`, `ARHGAP40`, `PLEC`, `SYT12`), `H2BC12` (`REG_e3aa30c984295df98692`, independently retained with missing vendor expression), and the in-frame `CDH1::NRG1` fusion (`REG_1f7babf47bbafeebeedc`). It also encodes the same `KCTD19 p.G920R` somatic event (`mn_chr16_67289592_C_T`), shortened from 25 aa (`REG_9dde223e9bf58444173b`, `MDLLIQRGLSRSVSYSILRKYLQED` in Designs 2 and 3) to 23 aa (`REG_6c716df1b65d721ee904`, `LLIQRGLSRSVSYSILRKYLQED` in Design 4 main, trimming two N-terminal unmutated residues `MD` while preserving all recorded target-overlapping peptides), adds `TJP2 p.S886P` (`REG_ef71940c452b9b68a401`, `25 aa`) and the unpadded C-terminal `FES p.R804W` window (`REG_0d4d002ad8a14e5a2f75`, `15 aa`), and omits Design 3's five reference-derived Guardant360 plasma VUS windows (`AR p.P555S`, `DHX36 p.R160K`, `ERBB4 p.D113V`, `KMT2D p.R2789Q`, `RAF1 p.G535S`), five junction-only RNA splice windows (`F5`, `HLA-B`, `HSF1`, `NBPF14`, `KMT2D`; versus Design 2's full-window splice set `F5`, `MUC5B`, `MUC6`, `MUC3A`, `HLA-B`), five intentional-self/CTA windows (`PRAME`, `SPATA31A6`, `LRRC36`, `YBX2`, `TEKT5`), and the alternative `CDH1--NRG1` fusion hypothesis (`REG_98875bfa1d09b7fab791`).

**Design 4 without KCTD19 is a separate 15-region construct alternative.** It removes that DNA-supported candidate because coding-strand mutant RNA is unconfirmed (`0` coding-strand and `2` opposite-strand mutant fragments in Jan 2025 liver WTS), then reoptimizes the order, linkers and known-segment PopRNA/GenBank nucleotide encoding (`481 aa`, `1,443 nt` sense coding, `1,586 nt` known RNA segment before unspecified PolyA). The column is not a list of exclusions, and neither option is established as clinically safer or ready for clinical use.

The eight Design 4 reserves (`SERPINA1`, `POLE`, `PRAME`, `NUTM1`, `MAGEA6`, `TSPYL6`, `MAGEA1`, `CDH1--NRG1`) come from its exploratory candidate pool; they are not simply the candidates removed from Design 2 or Design 3. Design 2 excluded `H2BC12` after unsuccessful expression-symbol checks, `TJP2`/readthrough for missing expression and ambiguous gene attribution, and `FES` for its shorter-than-25-aa window; Design 3 independently added `H2BC12` with missing expression while retaining the 25-aa window rule and adding five reference-derived Guardant360 VUS calls. In the three-BAM locus audit (`../exports/ctdna-locus-pileups.tsv`), none of those five Guardant360 VUS calls were called by paired Mutect2 in the Jan 2025 liver biopsy (`3/2,560` raw liver WES ALT/REF fragments for `KMT2D R2789Q`, `1/870` for `ERBB4 D113V`, and `0` for `AR P555S`, `DHX36 R160K` and `RAF1 G535S`; `1/13` and `1/168` coding-strand WTS ALT/REF fragments for `AR P555S` and `RAF1 G535S`, and `0` for the other three), and their lesion origin and transcription (`0.2%-0.4%` Oct 2025 cfDNA VAF for `DHX36 R160K`, `RAF1 G535S`, `ERBB4 D113V` and `AR P555S`; `3.2%` Oct 2025 and `0.2%` Mar 2025 cfDNA VAF for `KMT2D R2789Q`) remain unestablished.

## Class-I junction screen

A junction is an engineered join between encoded regions or linkers. A peptide-HLA occurrence is one positioned peptide evaluated for one allele; repeated sequences may count more than once. These rows concern joining peptides being screened, rather than intended tumour targets. For these junctions, a larger nM value means weaker predicted binding. Lower nM can be desirable when selecting an intended target; neither direction proves useful T-cell recognition.

## Additional predictors for the two Design 4 options

Designs 2 and 3 optimize junction order and linkers using a 4-phase MHCflurry Class-I affinity objective (in Design 3, MHCflurry Class-I presentation and CapHLA Class-II predictions are recorded as candidate annotations on source regions rather than junction optimization terms). Corresponding matched multi-model junction analyses were not freshly generated for every Design 2 or Design 3 junction query, so Designs 2 and 3 are not assigned zeros or ranked in this table. Class-II results in Design 4 use nine conditional HLA definitions with MixMHC2pred 2.1-beta1. Model percentile scales are not interchangeable or probabilities of response or harm.

The 15-region alternative is not uniformly preferable: its Class-I <=2% fraction and its total mild Class-II count are higher, although some other screen counts are lower. Removing KCTD19 removes an unconfirmed expression assumption and a potential target. No option has demonstrated better patient safety, cancer control or survival. Selection for further research requires scientific judgment, not choosing a column by its largest or smallest numbers.
