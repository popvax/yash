# Methods and evidence limits

## Source material and curation

The analysis uses frozen exports from [popvax/yash](https://github.com/popvax/yash), identified by commit and file hashes in [the source receipt](exports/source-receipt.json). Checked against upstream commit `7ece8f7a77c8db935422f6502d2c4db2352d7bf0`, 20 of its 36 records match upstream files byte for byte, one adapted script (`construct/mrna/run.py`) matches its upstream pre-adaptation source hash, and 15 refer to earlier/local research inputs; see [the source check](review/source-verification.json.gz). The source receipt's `adapted_after_snapshot` flag records editing after the local snapshot; it does not establish a match to upstream. Each record in that source check carries a `hash_target` field stating whether its recorded SHA-256 covers the stored bytes of `bundle_file` (`file_bytes`) or the decompressed payload of the gzipped file actually present (`decompressed_bytes`); both conventions are in use, and `verify_results.py` validates every record under its declared convention. The tumour specimen (liver biopsy Block A2, ~60–70% histological tumour cellularity) was collected on 15 January 2025; histological cellularity does not measure the malignant-versus-liver RNA transcript mixture fraction. Clinical report values from the four repository PDFs and post-report narrative/sample metadata (`data/clinical-timeline.md`, `data/samples.tsv`, `rnafoundry.com/yash/`) are separated by source in `review/clinical-reconciliation.json`: Guardant360 plasma cfDNA epigenomic tumour fraction rose from 1.4% (29 March 2025) to 31.9% (14 October 2025), and per project narrative notes clinical progression occurred in lung and peritoneum while liver and pancreatic lesions were described as controlled. Seven Guardant360-covered somatic alterations present in the January 2025 liver biopsy were also detected in October 2025 cfDNA (`CDH1::NRG1`, `TERT c.-124C>T`, `ROS1 F1699F`, `MERTK E192*`, `JUN R276*`, `RXRA A214S`, `TGFBR2 N515Kfs*26`), whereas 13 of Design 4's 15 somatic mutation genes are absent from Guardant360's targeted panel and remain unmeasured in October 2025 plasma and progressing lesions (`review/missing-data-priorities.tsv`). Current project metadata report matched-normal blood collection on 30 October 2026. The source exports do not establish November 2026 tumour expression or treatment status.

The curation script partitions 24 candidates into 16 encoded regions and eight reserves. The main construct retains the 12 paired-DNA mutation candidates from Design 2 and Design 3, adds H2BC12 (also included in Design 3), TJP2 and the observed 15-aa FES window, and includes one in-frame CDH1::NRG1 fusion window while omitting Design 3's five reference-derived Guardant360 VUS entries, five RNA-only splice windows, five intentional-self/CTA windows and second fusion hypothesis. All 15 mutation windows meet the recorded Class-I affinity nomination threshold of 500 nM. The fusion's approximately 509 nM prediction is admitted by an explicit driver-fusion exception.

Design 2 records H2BC12's exclusion as missing supplied expression after symbol checks (Design 3 subsequently admitted H2BC12 with missing expression), TJP2/readthrough exclusion as missing expression and ambiguous gene attribution, and FES exclusion as insufficient length for its 25-aa requirement. A successful legacy-symbol lookup is not established. A missing `supplied_gene_value` means the supplied gene-level expression value is unavailable, not that mutant RNA is absent. `original_RNA_gene_label` preserves source annotation; the TJP2 display label originates from `AL358113.1` and does not resolve its gene assignment. `coding_strand_spliced_reads_pm20kb` counts regional alignments within ±20 kb, not gene-specific or mutant-supporting fragments. TJP2 remains a display annotation for an identical observed peptide window with retained transcript/readthrough ambiguity.

FBXO11 `chr2:47813234 C>G` has somatic DNA support and 10 ALT fragments among 28 RNA fragments, but the supplied [RNA reconstruction record](exports/rna-evidence.json.gz) contains no mutant-protein hypothesis (`ALT_RNA_WITHOUT_RECONSTRUCTED_PROTEIN`). It therefore supplies no reconstructed mutant peptide window for this selection. Its [Atlas](alphagenome2/atlas-avi-somatic.tsv) and [conditioned splicing scores](alphagenome2/somatic-expression-splicing.tsv) remain recorded predictions: they neither explain the reconstruction failure nor establish a translated splice-derived antigen. Comparing scores requires matching the scorer, condition, track and units.

KCTD19 is shortened from 25 to 23 aa while preserving its recorded target-overlapping peptides; because its amino-acid window changed, it receives a distinct `region_id` (`REG_6c716df1b65d721ee904` versus Design 2 and Design 3's 25-aa `REG_9dde223e9bf58444173b`), while all 13 unchanged 25-aa regions shared with Design 3 (and 12 shared with Design 2) retain their upstream `region_id`s (`event_id` `mn_chr16_67289592_C_T` links both KCTD19 windows). The 16 regions comprise fourteen 25-aa regions, one 23-aa region and one 15-aa region: 388 aa in total, including the fusion. Patient-derived routing elements add 81 aa and the selected linkers add 38 aa, yielding 507 aa.

The sampling filters and specified window boundaries are curation choices, not calibrated safety or response boundaries. Passing them does not establish tumour presentation; changing a read-count threshold or shortening the selection does not establish a safer or more effective construct.

## Strand-resolved RNA evidence

The recorded orientation audit examined 22,733 spliced fragments across 200 loci; 22,197 were consistent with Read 1 antisense orientation (97.64%). A separate upstream 200-gene benchmark examined 1,275 fragments and found 98.20% agreement. These are separate sample sets. Orientation is inferred as `fr-firststrand`; the exact library chemistry is not established by this calculation.

Counts are based on fragment/read names under the documented alignment filters, not UMI molecules or independent cancer cells. Twelve core mutations have at least two coding-strand mutant fragments, summing to 234. FES and SYT12 each have one coding-strand and one opposite-strand fragment. KCTD19 has zero coding-strand and two opposite-strand mutant fragments. Total coding-strand and opposite-strand counts are 236 and nine. These sums are not expression measurements or tumour coverage.

The 543 spliced alignments near FES describe its ±20 kb neighborhood, which includes other genes. They are not 543 FES-specific or mutant-supporting fragments. Shallow coding-strand coverage neither confirms KCTD19 expression nor establishes its absence. A separately solved 481-aa construct (`strand-strict-15/`) excludes KCTD19 and reoptimizes both the remaining protein joins and its known-segment PopRNA/GenBank nucleotide encoding (`1,443 nt` sense coding, `1,586 nt` known RNA segment).

## HLA and prediction models

The six Class-I alleles used are HLA-A*01:01, A*33:03, B*08:01, B*15:08, C*01:02 and C*07:02. These agree with the predicted tumour-DNA Class-I genotypes in the Caris report, which recommends confirmation with an appropriate assay. MHCflurry 2.3.14 with the recorded 2.3.0 model bundle supplies affinity and processing/presentation predictions. Junction threshold counts use affinity percentiles. MixMHCpred 3.0 and PRIME 2.1 provide additional rankings; PRIME incorporates binding information, so agreement is not independent clinical validation.

MixMHC2pred 2.1-beta1 evaluates 15-mers with and without explicit flanking context. The nine conditional definitions are DRB1*03:01, DRB1*08:02, DRB3*02:02, DQA1*05:01–DQB1*02:01, DQA1*04:01–DQB1*04:02, DQA1*04:01–DQB1*02:01, DPA1*01:03–DPB1*02:01, DPA1*02:01–DPB1*13:01 and DPA1*01:03–DPB1*13:01. Pairing and surface expression are unproven; the combinations are incomplete. DRB3*02:02 follows the DNA call, whereas RNA typing reports *02:256.

Class-II context comprises three upstream residues, the first and last three residues of the peptide, and three downstream residues. Full-chain checks use actual sequence and terminal padding only at real termini. Some mutation-containing 15-mers have unchanged predicted binding cores; rankings do not establish mutation-specific CD4 recognition. All rankings and affinities are predictions, not response probabilities or binding measurements.

## Reference and homolog searches

Exact searches use GENCODE v46 and v50 provider translations (cited by EBI URL and SHA-256/MD5 checksums in `references/reference-receipt.json` and `references/v50-audit/reference-receipt.json`) and 112,389 constructed patient-normal coding/HLA contexts (`references/patient-normal-and-hla-contexts.fasta.gz`). The normal reference excludes 8,987 incomplete-CDS models and does not represent every possible normal protein. Reference absence is conditional on the query set and snapshots. The Class-I nominations contain 558 distinct target peptides, retained after exact-reference exclusion. The broader union of target-overlapping 8–11-mers and 15-mers contains 723 queries: eight normal NRG1 matches are excluded from target nomination, leaving 715 reference-absent queries. Normal flanks and routing domains remain encoded; the whole protein is not asserted to be absent from normal tissue.

The homolog scan enumerates matches within its defined mismatch, central-sequence and anchor rules for both constructs independently, retaining complete per-query coverage tables alongside filtered hit/binder subsets (`safety/` for the 16-region construct: `184` Class-I queries, `1,915` sense cryptic 8–11-mers [`36` with MHCflurry rank `<=0.5%` on each peptide's minimum-nM allele; `38` with `<=0.5%` on any of the six patient Class-I alleles], `197` Class-I and `39` Class-II controls; `strand-strict-15/safety/` for the 15-region alternative: `174` Class-I queries including all `24` alternative-specific junction queries, `1,782` sense cryptic 8–11-mers [`30` `<=0.5%` on the minimum-nM allele; `32` `<=0.5%` on any patient allele], `184` Class-I and `37` Class-II controls). It does not cover all structurally cross-reactive T-cell targets, as detailed in [the safety notes](SAFETY.md).

The defined mismatch/central-anchor search runs on all admitted Class-I queries: 128 targets plus 56 junction queries in the main construct, and 116 plus 58 in the alternative. The higher-priority `TIER2_TITIN_CLASS_LEAD_MIMIC` label is limited to lead peptides (19 main / 17 alternative pairs); non-lead mimics remain in the homolog tables under other categories. That lead-only prioritisation is a heuristic, not an omission from the search or a validated ordering of clinical risk. Higher DAI for a non-lead pair does not imply stronger absolute binding or greater cross-recognition.

`pipeline/alphagenome2_audit.py` (`--execute` with `alphagenome==2.0.0`, verified offline via `alphagenome2/additional-evidence-receipt.json`) separates inherited frozen tables from unconditionally re-executed live API tables, retains all target-gene per-track rows (matching the focal Ensembl ID or gene symbol, and strand-compatible for interval profiles) with explicit `imputation_mode` labels in `alphagenome2/variant-track-scores.tsv.gz` (`1,044` rows) and `alphagenome2/interval-track-scores.tsv.gz` (`3,292` rows), and notes that `supported_conditions()` (`alphagenome2/condition-imputation-metadata.tsv`) reports only the best channel status per output type in AlphaGenome 2's reference training corpus rather than all channels or patient-tissue measurements. Splicing variant scorers (`SPLICE_SITES`, `SPLICE_SITE_USAGE`, `SPLICE_JUNCTIONS`) are requested only on bulk-RNA reference tissues (`pancreas`, `liver`, `lung`) and never on single-cell-only conditions (`ductal`, `peritoneum`) where all splicing channels are `imputed_exploratory`; while `variant-track-scores.tsv.gz` retains every target-gene channel returned for those bulk tissues for auditability, summary columns in `alphagenome2/ctdna-progression-audit.tsv` filter strictly to `imputation_mode == "observed"` with separate unit-labelled columns (`obs_splice_sites_max_prob_diff`, `obs_splice_usage_max_frac_diff`, `obs_splice_junctions_max_log_diff`). All 13 Guardant360 locus pileups and MHCflurry Class-I predictions fed into `alphagenome2/ctdna-progression-audit.tsv` are reproducibly computed by `tools/audit_ctdna_loci.py` (`exports/ctdna-locus-pileups.tsv`, `exports/ctdna-mhcflurry-predictions.tsv`). Because AlphaGenome 2 `CellConditioningInput` expects integer single-cell UMI counts whereas `vendor-transformed-expression.tsv` is bulk FFPE WTS transformed expression from a mixed liver-metastasis biopsy, pseudo-UMI conditioning (`cosine_similarity = 0.996` against the bundled embedding) is an out-of-distribution exploratory prior rather than a single-cell malignant measurement. Reference-organ or Atlas predictions—including higher predicted `KCTD19` activity in reference testis than in reference pancreas/liver/lung, low predicted `ROS1 F1699F` observed splice-site deltas, `RAF1 G535S` `AlphaMissense = 0.970`, and `KMT2D p.R2789Q` (`c.8366G>A`, `AlphaMissense = 0.1775` alongside unverified reference splice predictions)—do not measure Yash's unbiopsied metastases, establish splice neutrality or normal-organ silence, or prove safety.

## Protein optimization

The solver uses the eleven options in [linkers.json](construct/linkers.json) and 2,992 recorded directed edge options. Complete internal antigen/linker windows are screened with the 19-aa Kyte–Doolittle heuristic at 1.6. Routing-domain overlaps are exported separately. The 15-aa FES region is individually unscorable by this heuristic; assembled windows and two-neighbor contexts are checked.

In each construct, six above-threshold 19-aa windows overlap the final linker/MITD boundary; five include part of FES's terminal `WPSFS` and all overlap the intended transmembrane MITD segment. They are retained as `ROUTING_BOUNDARY_REVIEW`, not omitted ([main windows](construct/protein/hydrophobicity-windows.tsv.gz), [alternative windows](strand-strict-15/construct/protein/hydrophobicity-windows.tsv.gz)). The full-chain scan covers 489 and 463 windows respectively. This policy does not establish membrane topology, solubility or correct processing; an individually unscorable 15-aa segment is not thereby demonstrated unsafe.

The ten lexicographic objectives minimize MHCflurry strong-rank rows, MixMHCpred strong-rank rows, conditional Class-II strong synthetic-core rows, PRIME low-rank rows, the combined mild-rank/500 nM burden, 500 nM rows, and MHCflurry mild-rank rows; then maximize the minimum MHCflurry percentile, minimize inserted residues, and maximize the quantized boundary-processing proxy. Each phase requires an optimal solver status and matching bound. The final proxy is derived from MHCflurry, not NetChop, and is not a measured cleavage probability. The recorded antigen order is in [components.tsv](construct/protein/components.tsv).

The processing predictor receives the complete selected 8–11-aa lead peptide with five-residue flanks. The `<5` tests identify flanks that depend on neighbouring regions; they do not clip the peptide to fewer than five residues. Only lead-peptide, assembly-dependent terms contribute to this final tie-breaker, not every encoded epitope. This is a limited model proxy, not a validated objective for tumour presentation or clinical benefit.

Optimality applies to this fixed search space and stored scores. Main and KCTD19-excluded proteins are checked in their actual assembled contexts, including all 493 and 467 15-mer occurrences. Mild synthetic Class-II row counts are 12 and 11; PRIME retains one low-rank junction row in each protein.

## RNA encoding and replay

The synonymous encoding uses the pinned codon table, backbone and motif rules. For the 16-region main construct, ninety-six initial alternative-frame stop intervals are protected; the final 1,521-nt sense coding sequence contains 98 alternative-frame stops, 56.1473% GC, a frozen-table CAI of 0.7722401, no repeated 12-nt pairs, a coding-RNA MFE of −553.0 kcal/mol and a coding-RNA longest paired-bracket run of 13 nt (`11 bp` uninterrupted MFE stem; across the 1,664-nt known RNA segment including fixed 5′/3′ UTRs, MFE is −613.3 kcal/mol, longest paired-bracket run is 22 nt, and longest uninterrupted MFE stem is `11 bp` / `15 bp` bulge-tolerant). The bounded rescue starts and ends at 13 nt on the coding sequence and evaluates zero candidate folds. Initiation-window and AUG marginal means remain unchanged at approximately 0.360834 and 0.356427. These are folding/accessibility heuristics, not expression or stability measurements.

Data labels and donor concordance do not authenticate named-patient chain of custody; see [provenance](PROVENANCE.md).

The nucleotide files for the 16-region main construct define a 1,521-nt sense coding sequence, 1,524-nt CDS including its first stop, 1,664-nt known RNA segment and 3,605-bp known DNA template segment (`48` GenBank features). The corresponding files for the 15-region KCTD19-excluded alternative (`strand-strict-15/construct/mrna/`) define a 1,443-nt sense coding sequence (`55.7866%` GC, `CAI = 0.78028203`, coding-RNA `MFE = -500.1 kcal/mol`, `0` repeated 12-nt pairs, `13-nt` coding-RNA longest paired-bracket run, `92` protected initial alternative-frame stops and `96` final alternative-frame stops), a 1,446-nt CDS including its first stop, a 1,586-nt known RNA segment (known-transcript `MFE = -564.3 kcal/mol`, `23-nt` known-transcript longest paired-bracket run, `9-bp` longest uninterrupted MFE stem / `14-bp` bulge-tolerant) and a 3,527-bp known DNA template segment (`46` GenBank features). PolyA, cap chemistry, nucleotide modifications and delivery remain unspecified in both options.

`verify_results.py` checks the file manifest and supplied invariants across both constructs and the recorded AlphaGenome 2 / clinical-reconciliation receipts; `--rescan-reference` repeats the three frozen reference searches. Compressed `.tsv.gz` tables are written with Python `gzip.compress(..., mtime=0)`, which removes header-timestamp drift within a given Python/`zlib` build, though gzip OS-header bytes (`RFC 1952` byte 9) or deflate bitstreams can still differ across distinct `gzip`/`zlib` implementations if `.gz` tables are regenerated from scratch. Full replay (`run.py`) re-derives both selections, re-establishes the ten stored objective optima for both constructs, assembles both canonical proteins and reruns both RNA encodings. Sequence and annotation files require exact hashes. Diagnostic floats use `1e-12` relative and absolute tolerances with exact discrete types. Replay does not repeat raw-read analysis, model inference or experiments. See [the pipeline guide](pipeline/README.md) for entry points.
